# Premiere (Season 1, Episode 1)

## Overview
"Premiere" is the pilot episode of the *Farscape* television series, created by Rockne S. O'Bannon.[1] It was co-produced by The Jim Henson Company and Hallmark Entertainment.[2] It originally aired in North America on the Sci-Fi Channel on March 19, 1999.[3] Notably, it is the only episode in the series whose opening credits do not feature John Crichton's standard voiceover.

## Production Background
The series genesis dates back to 1992, when Brian Henson and The Jim Henson Company sought to create a television showcase for their advanced creature and animatronic capabilities.[1] They initially envisioned a high-stakes alien environment akin to the *Star Wars* Mos Eisley cantina.[3] Because building elaborate alien sets and animatronics for a single standalone pilot was financially prohibitive, the Henson Company required commitment for a full series order before production could begin. Consequently, "Premiere" was not a pilot in the traditional sense, and to save production costs, it was filmed concurrently with the second episode, "I, E.T."

## Plot Summary
International Aeronautics and Space Administration scientist Commander John Crichton tests his experimental module, *Farscape One*, using an atmospheric slingshot maneuver near Earth.[1] The module is struck by a radiation wave and sucked through a wormhole into the Uncharted Territories, landing directly in the middle of a space battle.[3] Crichton witnesses a vessel get destroyed after clipping his module and is pulled aboard *Moya*, a living Leviathan starship recently liberated by escaped prisoners: Ka D'Argo, Pa'u Zotoh Zhaan, and Dominar Rygel XVI.

The crew injects Crichton with translator microbes so he can communicate.[3] During their chaotic escape jump, a Peacekeeper Prowler and its pilot, Officer Aeryn Sun, are inadvertently dragged along with them.[1] Aeryn is subsequently declared irreversibly contaminated by her commanding officer, Captain Bialar Crais, after contacting Crichton and the alien prisoners. Crais pursues *Moya* relentlessly, seeking vengeance for the accidental death of his younger brother, Tauvo Crais, who was killed when his ship clipped *Farscape One* during Crichton's arrival.[3] Utilizing his own flight trajectory theories originally developed for *Farscape One*, Crichton helps *Moya* evade Crais's Command Carrier, cementing his uneasy place among the fugitive crew.

## Canon & Continuity Notes
- First Appearances: Introduces John Crichton, Aeryn Sun, Ka D'Argo, Pa'u Zotoh Zhaan, Rygel XVI, Pilot, Moya, Captain Bialar Crais, and Jack Crichton.
- The Wormhole Inciting Incident: Establishes the central premise of Crichton's exile and the Peacekeeper obsession with wormhole technology.
- Production Detail: The shuttle launch sequence at the beginning features the module bearing the insignia Collaroy, referencing a suburb of Sydney, Australia where the series was filmed.[1]
