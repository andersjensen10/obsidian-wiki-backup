# Durka Returns

> **Scope:** bounded first-pass episode dossier focused on the episode's canon events, Chiana's introduction, Nebari mind cleansing as presented here, and the production history of the ending. This is not a full scene-by-scene transcript audit.

## Episode identity

“Durka Returns” is the fifteenth episode of season one, written by Grant McAloon and directed by Tony Tilse; the production number is 10115 and the U.S. airdate is listed as 13 August 1999.[2][3] The episode follows [[Moya]] after a damaged starburst brings a Nebari transport aboard, carrying the apparently rehabilitated Peacekeeper Captain Selto Durka and the prisoner [[Chiana]].[1][2]

## Canon in the episode

- [[Rygel XVI]] identifies the returned Durka as his former torturer; Salis, the Nebari official, insists that Durka has been mentally cleansed and is no longer dangerous.[1][3]
- Chiana is held in a control collar and says that the Nebari intend to perform mental cleansing on her. Her resistance establishes the procedure as a threat to identity and dissent in the episode's immediate canon, while later details belong in the planned [[Mind Cleansing]] and [[Nebari]] notes.[1][3]
- Salis's death releases Durka's previous personality. The crew must regain control of Moya while Chiana escapes confinement, bargains with Rygel, and ultimately helps the crew oppose Durka.[1][2]
- Chiana asks to remain with Moya's crew after the crisis. The final exchange leaves her involvement in Salis's murder deliberately unresolved: when Crichton asks where she was, she answers with a grin rather than a confession.[1][4]
- For a freely available viewing lead, the indexed YouTube upload is **Farscape S1E15 FULL Episode | Durka Returns**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCs0-r2YauA. The retrieved transcript exposes an opening sequence around **00:07–05:08** covering Moya's impaired starburst, the boarding of the Nebari transport, Rygel's recognition of Durka, and Salis's explanation of the cleansing; a complete timestamp audit remains outstanding.[6]

## Production and interpretation boundaries

Chiana was initially intended as a guest character and was scripted to die from Durka's pulse weapon. Gigi Edgley and production reporting describe a revision in which the shot grazes her, followed by added material showing Zhaan treating the wound; the later recurring/regular status is therefore a production-history fact, not something the episode itself explains.[4][5]

The episode supports reading Chiana as frightened, oppositional, improvisational, and unwilling to submit to the Nebari procedure. It does **not**, by itself, settle whether she killed Salis, provide a complete account of Nebari society, or establish every later detail of her history. The fan-maintained episode page and recap are useful for locating scene and interpretation leads, but important canon claims here are limited to the episode/archived broadcaster account and cross-checked secondary sources.[1][3][7]
