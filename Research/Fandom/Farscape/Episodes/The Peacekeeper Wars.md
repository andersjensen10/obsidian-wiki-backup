# The Peacekeeper Wars

## Overview

*Farscape: The Peacekeeper Wars* is a three-part miniseries that serves as the definitive conclusion to the Farscape television series. It was produced after the show's cancellation and aired in 2004 as a made-for-TV event, providing closure to the major storylines involving the wormhole knowledge, the Scarran-Peacekeeper war, and the fate of the Moya crew.[1]

## Production background

Following the cancellation of Season 4 in 2003, fan campaigns and network interest led to the production of *The Peacekeeper Wars* as a three-hour miniseries. It was produced by Hallmark Entertainment and premiered on the Sci Fi Channel (now SYFY) in the United States in 2004.[2]

The miniseries brought together the full principal cast and was directed by Robert C. Cooper, who had directed several episodes during the series' run. The production was notable for utilizing the Homebush Studios facilities in Sydney, Australia, the same facility used for the main series.[2]

## Synopsis

*The Peacekeeper Wars* is structured as three sequential parts (often referred to as Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3 or Parts 1–3). The overarching plot follows these major threads:

### Scorpius's arc
Scorpius is removed from Peacekeeper power by the Harmony (a Peacekeeper secret police force), captured by the Scarrans, and eventually returns to lead the Peacekeeper forces. His transformation from antagonist to reluctant ally continues, and he plays a critical role in the final confrontation.[3]

### The Scarran-Peacekeeper war
A full-scale war erupts between the Peacekeeper and Scarran Empires. The war has been building throughout the series and comes to a climax during the miniseries, with the combined forces of the Farscape crew and various factions confronting the Scarran threat.[4]

### Crichton's wormhole knowledge
The central plot of *The Peacekeeper Wars* resolves the series' long-running wormhole storyline. Crichton ultimately uses the wormhole knowledge to create a self-sustaining wormhole — described as a "self-sustaining wormhole capable of swallowing the entire galaxy" — which forces both the Peacekeepers and Scarrans to negotiate a multilateral peace treaty. This demonstration reveals that the power of wormhole technology is beyond the control of any single lifeform.[4]

### Moya and the crew
Moya and her crew play a pivotal role in the events. Moya's connection to her Pilot Synap is explored, and the crew works together to influence the outcome of the war and secure peace.[5]

## Episode breakdown (three parts)

- **Part 1** — Sets up the post-Season 4 status quo. Scorpius's capture and the war's escalation. The crew learns of the Scarran advance and prepares for the final confrontation.
- **Part 2** — The war reaches full intensity. Multiple battle fronts are established. Character arcs converge as the crew and their allies prepare for the decisive confrontation.
- **Part 3** — The climactic battle and resolution. Crichton's wormhole demonstration forces peace. The crew's fates are resolved.

## Cast

The principal cast returns from the main series: Ben Browder (John Crichton), Claudia Black (Aeryn Sun), Gina Torres (Chiana), Anthony Simcoe (D'Argo), Virginia Hey (Rygel XVI), Barry Humphries (voice of Stakk), Keith Scott (Stark), and Wayne Pygram (Scorpius). Guest cast includes actors portraying Harmony members, Scarran commanders, and Peacekeeper personnel.[2]

Dave Elsey and his Creature Shop team returned for prosthetics and creature work, consistent with the series' established production standards.[2]

## Legacy and conclusion

*The Peacekeeper Wars* is widely regarded by fans and critics as a satisfying conclusion to the series. It resolves the central wormhole plot, the Scarran-Peacekeeper war, and most major character arcs. However, it does not resolve every thread — notably, the ultimate fate of the Leviathans and the Ancients' ongoing presence in the universe remain open-ended.[1]

A follow-up miniseries, *Farscape: The Relic Wars*, was later released (2019–2020) to continue the story.
