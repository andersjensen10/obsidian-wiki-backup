# Alternate Reality and Unrealized Versions of Chiana

## Overview

Farscape features several alternate versions of Chiana across unrealized realities, dream sequences, and virtual realities. These are non-canonical variants that reveal different facets of the character.

## Alternate Versions

### Jessica (alternate identity)

An alternate version of Chiana appears under the name "Jessica" in one of the unrealized realities. Specific details need episode verification.

### Chiana/Aeryn

- **Episodes**: "Unrealized Reality" (4.11), "Prayer" (4.18)
- **Description**: Chiana/Aeryn existed in one of the unrealized realities Crichton saw while being shown the dangers of wormhole travel by the Ancient "Einstein." She acted like a blend of the two characters.

### Chiana the Witch

A version of Chiana appears as "Chiana the Witch" in the *Look at the Princess* trilogy. Specific details need episode verification.

### Noranti/Chiana

- **Episode**: "Unrealized Reality" (4.11)
- **Description**: This version existed in one of the unrealized realities Crichton saw. Though she had Chiana's facial shape, her overall appearance and personality was that of Noranti's.

### Unrealized Heavy Chiana

A version from the unrealized realities; specific details need episode verification.

### Farscape: Gone and Back — Married Chiana (Comic)

- **Source**: Volume 3 *Gone and Back* (BOOM! Studios, 2009)
- **Description**: Crichton encountered this Chiana after being pulled onto an unrealized version of Moya while visiting the planet Hyneria. She shared many similarities to his reality's Chiana, with the major exceptions that:
  - She was happily married to D'Argo
  - She was a rather talented cook
  - She had put on weight in her marital bliss

This comic version is particularly significant because it represents Chiana in a domesticated, settled state — the exact opposite of her core identity as a self-determined exile.

### John Quixote — Virtual Reality Chiana

- **Episode**: 4.07 "John Quixote"
- **Description**: Crichton and Chiana enter a bizarre virtual reality game where they encounter strange/twisted versions of the people they know. The "pseudo Chiana" appears as part of the VR distortion, alongside reversed versions of other characters (exploding Rygels, D'ArHansel, etc.).

## Significance

These alternate versions collectively test different aspects of Chiana's identity:
- **Domestication** (married to D'Argo, weight gain) — the life she rejected
- **Blending** (Chiana/Aeryn) — what if Chiana had Aeryn's military upbringing?
- **Role swap** (Noranti/Chiana) — identity as costume
- **VR distortion** (John Quixote) — identity as a game piece

## Evidence gaps
