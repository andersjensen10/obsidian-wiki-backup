# I-Yensch, You-Yensch

> Season 3, episode 19; production no. 10319. Written by Matt Ford and directed by Peter Andrikidis.[3]

## Episode identity

The episode aired in the UK on 21 January 2002 and in the US on 5 April 2002.[3] Its two interlocking plots are a negotiation between D'Argo, Rygel, and Scorpius at a remote diner, and the crisis caused by Talyn's escalating panic and violence.[3]

## Canon

### The Scorpius negotiation

D'Argo and Rygel meet Scorpius to arrange an alliance of convenience. They offer Crichton's wormhole expertise in exchange for amnesty, transport aboard Scorpius's Command Carrier, and safe relocation or return for the crew.[3] Their stated justification is that the Charrid–Scarran alliance is pursuing wormhole technology, and that Scorpius is preferable to allowing the alliance to obtain it.[3]

Scorpius proposes I-Yensch bracelets as a security measure. The devices synchronize the wearers' nerve impulses: pain inflicted on one is shared by the other, and the death of one would kill both.[3] D'Argo and Braca demonstrate the device, but Rygel rejects the proposed pairings unless Scorpius himself is exposed to the same risk.[3]

The negotiation is interrupted when Sko and Wa raid the diner. The situation reveals that the pair were hired by Voodi to burn the diner for insurance money, but they turn the arson into a hostage-taking scheme.[3] Rygel and Scorpius cooperate to manipulate the inexperienced robbers. Rygel uses the single-shot pulse charge Scorpius gives him, while Scorpius and the others overcome the attackers; the diner burns, and Rygel and D'Argo return to Moya claiming that the deal has been secured.[3]

### Talyn's collapse

Naj Gil is collected by a medical transport, and Jool considers leaving with him. Chiana stops her after experiencing a premonition of mourning over Jool; the episode presents the warning as part of Chiana's continuing precognitive ability, not as an explanation of the wider crisis.[3]

Talyn destroys two approaching Peacekeeper vessels and then fires on the departing medical ship, killing approximately 600 people aboard.[3] Crais argues that Talyn's panic and paranoia make him a danger to himself and others, and proposes shutting down his mechanical systems so that his biological components can later be repaired and fitted with a replacement system.[3]

Moya initially resists the plan, and Talyn subsequently fires on Moya after allowing John, Aeryn, and Crais to board. Aeryn reframes the shutdown as treatment rather than punishment; Talyn retracts his weapons, and the crew proceeds to shut him down.[3] The episode therefore treats the end of Talyn's existing personality as a tragic medical and ethical decision, while leaving the promised future restoration unresolved for the following arc.[3]

### John and Aeryn

The episode keeps John and Aeryn emotionally estranged after the events surrounding the two Crichtons. Aeryn says they can still work together, and at the end insists that they began the campaign together and will finish it together.[3] This is episode evidence for renewed operational cooperation, not a full resolution of their personal conflict.

## Production and reception context

The BBC's contemporary episode guide describes the diner plot as a failed or disrupted negotiation and emphasizes Talyn's hospital-ship attack as the episode's major escalation.[2] Reactor's later rewatch separates the episode's two plots and reads the diner hostage situation as the mechanism through which Rygel and Scorpius develop mutual trust; that is interpretation rather than in-universe fact.[1]

The fan-maintained Farscape Encyclopedia Project records a detailed cast and production roster, including the credited guest roles of Braca, Sko, Wa, Essk, Voodi, and Naj Gil, while also noting that Jool appears despite a credit anomaly.[3] The roster should be reconciled against screen credits before being treated as authoritative.
