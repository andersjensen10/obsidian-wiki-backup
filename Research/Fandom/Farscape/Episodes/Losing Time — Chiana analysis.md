# Losing Time (3.09) — Chiana analysis

## Plot summary

Energy riders with hidden agendas occupy the bodies of Moya's crew. Chiana is possessed by one of these energy riders, and after the rider is removed from her body, she begins experiencing flashes of precognition. [1]

## Chiana-specific significance

- **Precognition arc**: The possession event initiates a multi-episode precognition arc where Chiana's flashes grow in frequency and detail, eventually allowing her to perceive some events hours before they occur and change them before they're finalized.
- **Loss of agency**: Chiana's body is taken over by an external force — a thematic inversion of her character's core identity as someone who controls her own survival.
- **Post-possession change**: The precognition abilities persist after the rider is removed, suggesting a fundamental alteration in Chiana's perception.

## Precognition arc episodes

Chiana's precognition carries through multiple subsequent episodes:
- 3.11 "Incubator" — flashes continue
- 3.13 "Scratch 'N' Sniff" — precognition is plot-relevant
- 3.16 "Revenging Angel" — precognition arc
- 3.18 "Fractures" — precognition continues
- 3.19 "I-Yensch, You-Yensch" — precognition present
- 3.20 "Into the Lion's Den: Lambs to the Slaughter" — precognition present
