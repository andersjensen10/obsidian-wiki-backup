# Chiana screenshots — provenance

## What this record covers

A set of **28 locally stored screenshots of Chiana** (performer: Gigi Edgley) extracted from the broadcast episodes, saved in this `Media/` folder. This is the first locally archived still-image set for the Farscape slice; the earlier `[[Chiana makeup test provenance]]` record deliberately archived nothing and kept remote links only. The handling decision differs here because these are frames taken from episode footage already held locally for research, with a per-image timecode trail, rather than third-party promotional or behind-the-scenes media of unresolved authorship.

## Source

- Video source: complete-season copies of the series published on the Internet Archive by uploader **TVSTAR**, in the items `farscape-s-01`, `farscape-s-02`, and `farscape-s-04`. [1][2][3]
- Item titles as declared: “Farscape The Complete Season 1”, “Farscape Season 2”, “Farscape Season 4”; declared date field “1999”; media type `movies`; collections `classic_tv_1990s`, `classic_tv`, `television`.
- Files used, at `https://archive.org/download/<item>/<file>`:
  - `farscape-s-01/Farscape S01E15 Durka Returns.mp4`
  - `farscape-s-02/Farscape S02E01 Mind the Baby.mp4`
  - `farscape-s-02/Farscape S02E03 Taking the Stone.mp4`
  - `farscape-s-02/Farscape S02E18 A Clockwork Nebari.mp4`
  - `farscape-s-04/Farscape S04E08 I Shrink Therefore I Am.mp4`
  - `farscape-s-04/Farscape S04E14 Twice Shy.mp4`
- Delivered video resolution, from the item metadata: 624–640 × 480 for the season 1–2 files and 854 × 480 for the season 4 files (h.264, ~260–283 MB per episode).
- **Rights: unresolved.** No licence is declared on these Internet Archive items. They are third-party uploads of copyrighted television episodes; the uploader is not the rightsholder. The episodes themselves remain the property of the Jim Henson Company and the relevant production and distribution rightsholders included in the on-screen credits. These still images are held locally as research reference material only and are not for redistribution or publication.
- **Fair-use rationale for local storage.** The vault's default is to link or embed remote media rather than duplicate copyrighted files locally ([[../Farscape — Wiki Home]], editorial standards). This set departs from that default on AJ's explicit instruction, and it does so under a narrow rationale: these are single frames of a copyrighted work already held locally for research, used to support identification analysis of one character's appearance, makeup and costume across episodes; the set is a small fraction of the underlying works; and the provenance record preserves source, timecode and rightsholder status so the material can be removed or replaced with an authorised source later. If a licensed release is ever captured, the frames in this folder should be regenerated from it.
- Sources: [[../Sources/Farscape — Source Register]] records this source as FS-S096.

## Method

1. The six episodes above were extracted at **1 frame per second** with `ffmpeg`, giving 16,811 frames.
2. Each frame was machine-scored for a Chiana appearance signature: desaturated (grey/blue-white) skin, bright silver-white hair, and dark eyes, brows, lips and eye make-up inside the same face-sized region. Warm-toned Sebacean/human skin was penalised as a discriminator, and a multi-scale search was used so she is also found in medium and wide shots.
3. Candidates were de-duplicated in time (one pick per window) and assembled into labelled contact sheets, which were then **reviewed visually, cell by cell**. Frames that did not actually contain Chiana — title/credit cards, other characters, hardware, bright empty backgrounds — were rejected at this stage.
4. Every image kept was then re-checked **individually at full frame size** to confirm both identity and usability (face visible, in focus, adequately lit) before being written into this folder.

Consequence of step 3 worth recording: several high-scoring candidates were credit cards or shots of other characters, and two frames initially captioned as Chiana at 2.03 15m32s and 16m32s turned out on full-size inspection to be Aeryn Sun. They were removed and replaced with the correct frames at 16m34s and 16m41s.

## Observations from these frames

- The sampled frames show the character's **appearance varying materially by episode**. In 1.15, 2.18, 4.08 and 4.14 the hair reads as silver-white or platinum; in 2.03 it reads with a **red/pink cast**, and the 2.18 frames show a gold ornate collar and a silver-blue bodice that do not recur in the 4.08 and 4.14 frames. These are observations from this frame set, not production statements; the production account behind the design and per-season changes belongs in [[../Production/Chiana makeup and costume]] and needs credit-level sourcing.
- In 2.18 she is repeatedly shown **chained and collared**; the frames were selected from the mind-cleansing/rescue sequence and should be read as one scene cluster rather than as separate appearances.
- These frames are **standard-definition broadcast captures** (634–854 × 480). They are the ceiling of the source: no upscaling was applied and none should be implied. Where a larger image is wanted, the DVD/Blu-ray releases would need to be captured directly rather than these Internet Archive files.
