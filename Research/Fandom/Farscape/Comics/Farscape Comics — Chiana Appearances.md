# Farscape Comics — Chiana Appearances (Post-TV)

## Canon status

The BOOM! Studios *Farscape* comic series (2008–2011) is **officially canon** per the Jim Henson Company. Written by series creator Rockne S. O'Bannon and novelist Keith R.A. DeCandido. Each 4-issue mini-arc functions as a "season 5" episode. [1]

## Main series volumes and Chiana presence

### Volume 1: *The Beginning of the End of the Beginning* (Dec 2008 – Apr 2009)
- Issues: #1 "Return of the King" → #4 "Hynerian Rhapsody"
- Plot: Moya takes Rygel to Hyneria for his throne; things go wrong.
- **Chiana presence**: Not named in summary; appears as part of Moya's crew but no specific plot focus noted.

### Volume 2: *Strange Detractors* (Apr 2009 – Jul 2009)
- Issues: #1 "Leave It to Crichton" → #4 "The Binds that Tie"
- Plot: A strange disease sets the crew of Moya against each other.
- **Chiana presence**: Part of the ensemble cast; the disease likely affects her, but no specific focus.

### Volume 3: *Gone and Back* (Jul 2009 – Oct 2009)
- Issues: #1 "Through a Glass, Weirdly" → #4 "All Fall Down"
- Plot: Crichton enters an Unrealized Reality where Zhaan and D'Argo are alive but he and Aeryn never met.
- **Chiana presence**: Appears in the primary reality (not the Unrealized Reality) while Crichton is elsewhere. No specific focus noted.

### Volume 4: *Tangled Roots* (Nov 2009 – Feb 2010)
- Issues: #1–#4 (ongoing series)
- Plot: **Crichton and Chiana go after Roiin**, the bounty hunter chasing Deke, while Aeryn pursues her own past.
- **Chiana presence: Co-lead with Crichton.** This is the most significant Chiana-focused comic arc — she is a primary protagonist alongside Crichton, tracking a bounty hunter across space.

### Volume 5+:
- Subsequent ongoing series issues exist (up to #24+). Chiana continues as a series-regular crew member in the post-*Peacekeeper Wars* timeline.

## Key finding

**Volume 4: *Tangled Roots*** is the first post-TV comic where Chiana is explicitly named as a co-lead with Crichton. This is a rare instance where Chiana carries the primary plot burden in a non-television medium.

## Evidence gaps

- Scene-by-scene Chiana presence in Volumes 1–3 not verified from the actual comics.
- Volumes 5+ (ongoing series through #24) need issue-level review for Chiana focus.
- *Farscape Uncharted Tales* — anthology format, Chiana appearances unknown.
- *Farscape: Scorpius* miniseries — Chiana presence unknown (Scorpius-centric; Chiana may appear minimally).
- Anniversary / special issues need review.
- No local copy of any comic is archived; this is a web-index-based first pass.
