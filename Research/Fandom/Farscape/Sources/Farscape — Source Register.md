## Source handling rules

- Record the exact URL, title, publisher/channel, creator when known, publication date when known, and access date.
- For video sources, record the exact video URL, timestamp range, transcript status, and episode/topic mapping.
- For images, record creator/rightsholder, source URL, license or fair-use rationale, and whether the file is linked, embedded, or locally stored.
- Do not cite a search-result snippet as if it were the source page.
- Keep conflicting claims visible and link each claim to its supporting sources.
