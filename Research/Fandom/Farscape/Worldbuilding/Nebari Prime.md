# Nebari Prime

[[Nebari Prime]] is the homeworld of [[Characters/Chiana]] and her brother Nerri; the available reference material treats it as the likely homeworld of the wider Nebari species.[3] The series does not provide a complete geographic or civic description of the planet in the material reviewed for this note.[unverified]

## Political setting

The clearest episode-grounded information about Nebari Prime comes from “A Clockwork Nebari.”

Chiana says that she and Nerri wanted to leave the planet when they were young, but the government issued them official exit permits rather than preventing their departure.[1][4]

Their apparent escape was therefore part of the Establishment’s covert contagion program: they had been infected during a routine physical examination and released as unwitting carriers.[1][4]

The governing body is called the Establishment. Its policy is presented as an authoritarian program of social control: dissent is treated through mental cleansing, while Varla frames submission as a choice between cleansing and death.[1][2]

The episode’s dialogue and surrounding reference material support describing this as coercive state policy, not as a neutral medical service.[1][2]

The Establishment’s external plan was to seed a dormant, sexually transmissible contagion among people leaving Nebari Prime and other selected populations.

The intended result was enough social chaos, once activated, to reduce resistance when Nebari emissaries expanded into affected regions.[1][2][4]

The transcript archive identifies the episode as written by Lily Taylor and directed by Rowan Woods, with a US airdate of 15 September 2000.[1]

## Chiana and Nerri

Chiana and Nerri are the note’s best-documented personal connection to Nebari Prime.

Chiana describes them as young people who wanted to leave; after two cycles, Nerri discovered the purpose of their permits and obtained an antibody from someone high in the Establishment.[1][4]

He then separated from Chiana to protect the identity of that source and became associated with the Nebari resistance.[1][2]

The episode reveals Nerri through a message and reports of resistance activity rather than through a return to the planet. Accordingly, Nerri’s leadership role and the resistance’s existence are canon claims in the episode, while the organization’s scale, structure, and location remain unspecified here.[1][2]

## What is and is not established

**Established or strongly supported:**

- Nebari Prime is Chiana and Nerri’s homeworld.[1][3]
- Exit permits were used to move selected people off-world as contagion carriers.[1][4]
- The Establishment governed Nebari society and used mental cleansing against dissent.[1][2]
- The contagion operation was designed to support later Nebari expansion.[1][2][4]

**Not established by the reviewed sources:**

- A map, capital city, named regions beyond the later reference to Walapawn province, or a reliable physical description of the planet.[2][unverified]
- The full constitutional structure of the Establishment, its relationship to the resistance, or the identity of the official who supplied Nerri’s antibody.[1][2][unverified]
- The exact size of the Nebari population or the geographic reach of Nebari control.[unverified]
- A production-source description of Nebari Prime’s sets, locations, or visual design.[unverified]

The fan-maintained Nebari Prime entry is useful for the planet’s basic identity but is only one sentence long.[3]

The transcript archive preserves dialogue and episode metadata, but it credits an older transcript archive rather than presenting an official studio script.[1]

The Companion article supplies retrospective production context for Chiana’s backstory and identifies Nebari Prime as the character’s homeworld, but it is not a primary episode transcript.[5]
