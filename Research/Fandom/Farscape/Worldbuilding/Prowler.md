# Prowler

## Overview

The Prowler is the Peacekeeper Empire's primary fighter craft — a single-pilot, atmospheric-capable starfighter used for reconnaissance, dogfighting, transport-pod operations, and deep-space pursuit. The Prowler is one of the most recognizable ship designs in Farscape, featured in nearly every episode as the standard Peacekeeper combat craft.[1]

## Technical characteristics

- **Role:** Single-pilot fighter craft (interceptor, pursuit, escort).
- **Maneuverability:** Highly maneuverable for a Peacekeeper craft; can operate both in atmosphere and in vacuum.
- **Armament:** Standard Prowlers carry forward-facing weapons (laser/laser-cannon types) and are capable of engaging Leviathans at close range, though they are outmatched by Leviathan-scale defense screens.
- **Pilot complement:** One pilot, seated in a forward-facing cockpit.
- **Design:** Distinctive Peacekeeper angular aesthetic with a pointed nose, swept-back wings, and a single-seat configuration. Typically painted in the standard Peacekeeper grey/black livery.

## Canonical appearances

- **Episode 1.01 "Premiere"** — Crichton is captured by a Peacekeeper Prowler and taken to the command carrier. This establishes the Prowler as a key visual element of the Peacekeeper military presence.
- **Season 1 through 4** — Prowlers appear regularly in pursuit scenes, dogfight sequences, and as the standard Peacekeeper deployment craft from carrier ships. They are the visible arm of Peacekeeper military power across the series.
- **The Peacekeeper Wars** — Prowlers participate in the large-scale Peacekeeper/Scarran fleet battles.

## Prowler vs. Leviathan

Prowlers are the standard Peacekeeper interceptor, while the Leviathan is a larger biological transport. Prowlers cannot match a Leviathan's defense screens but are capable of coordinated attacks. The Prowler's agility contrasts with the Leviathan's Starburst speed — the Peacekeepers use them to pursue and intercept Leviathans.[2]
