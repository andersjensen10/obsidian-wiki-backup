# Leviathan (spacecraft)

## Overview

Leviathans are massive biomechanoid starships created by the ancient Builders. They are the largest and most technologically sophisticated vessels seen in Farscape — living ships that grow, feel, reproduce, and possess their own consciousness.[1][2] No two Leviathans are identical: as they grow, their internal structure and facilities develop to suit the needs of their crew.[1]

## Classification and design

- **Class:** Transport
- **Manufacturer:** The Builders (an ancient, now-extinct species)
- **Size:** Leviathans are enormous — described as having thick outer skin, internal atmospheres, command centers, living quarters, workshops, and hangar decks. The "Pilot's Den" is located at the very core of the ship.[2]
- **Type:** Bio-mechanical — composed of both biological and mechanical systems.[2]
- **Original role:** Ambassadors of peace, created to travel between civilizations and foster understanding.[1]
- **Peacekeeper exploitation:** The Peacekeepers captured and enslaved Leviathans, fitting them with control collars and using them as warships and transports against their nature.

## Internal structure

A Leviathan's interior includes:

- **Command center:** Located at the core, with a viewing port looking down through multiple tiers.
- **Pilot's Den:** At the base of the command systems, where the Pilot bonds neurally to the Leviathan's nervous system.[2]
- **Starburst chamber:** A specialized chamber at the Leviathan's core used for Starburst activation. Pilots cannot sense or eavesdrop on this chamber.[1]
- **Living quarters:** Accommodating as many as 150 crew, though Leviathans "prefer" fewer.[1]
- **Workshops, hangar decks, life-support chambers:** Developing organically as the Leviathan grows.[1]
- **Utility tiers:** Internal chambers typically inaccessible to passengers.
- **Air-flow shafts and fluid channels:** For the Leviathan's bodily functions and DRD travel.[1]

## Biology and physiology

### Outer systems

- **Thick skin:** Capable of maintaining a pressurized atmosphere inside.[2]
- **Bioelectric field:** Keeps small hull openings from venting atmosphere.[2]
- **Self-repair (Hodian trill bats):** Symbiotic organisms that excrete a sealant used to repair hull breaches.[2]

### Inner systems

- **Life support:** Maintained by the bonded Pilot; major failures occur if the Pilot is incapacitated.[2]
- **DRD manufacturing:** Leviathans produce their own Diagnostic Repair Drones (DRDs) — terrier-sized service robots that maintain the ship. DRDs are extensions of the Leviathan's consciousness when the Pilot is not controlling them.[3]
- **Neural interface:** The Pilot's nerve tendrils connect directly to the Leviathan's nervous system, creating a shared consciousness. The Pilot feels what the Leviathan feels and speaks for her when she cannot communicate directly.[4]

### Starburst

A Leviathan's primary defensive and long-range travel mechanism. Starburst involves riding an energy stream through another dimension, emerging at a random location.[1] It consumes significant energy, requiring the Leviathan to replenish before Starbursting again. When the Peacekeepers suppressed Moya's Starburst, it was not merely disabling a mechanical function — it was silencing a fundamental expression of a living being's autonomy.[4]

### Reproduction

Female Leviathans can gestate embryos internally, with the embryo nesting in one of the innermost chambers (usually inaccessible utility tiers). DRDs instinctively defend the developing child, sometimes attacking crew members who approach too closely. Side effects of pregnancy include involuntary Starbursts, life-support malfunctions, and erratic DRD behavior. Crews of pregnant Leviathans are usually advised to abandon ship.[2]

## Peacekeeper Leviathan program

The Peacekeepers established a Leviathan breeding program to produce a hybrid Leviathan warship that could take orders directly from a Sebacean captain without requiring a bonded Pilot. They successfully artificially impregnated Moya using Peacekeeper gunship (Dakkita-class) genetic material, producing the first hybrid: Talyn.[2]

All other attempts were with enslaved Leviathans wearing control collars. Talyn is a unique hybrid — part Leviathan, part Peacekeeper gunship — with a powerful external retractable gun turret and wall-mounted security guns. He doesn't need a Pilot to control himself, but has a vestigial Pilot's Den that can accommodate a bonded Pilot (he later bonds with Bialar Crais via a special neural implant at the top of his spine). Talyn also temporarily bonded with Stark while Crais was incapacitated.[2]

## Leviathan species inventory

Only five Leviathans are known to have been produced by the Builders:[1]

1. **Moya** — The series' primary vessel. Originally enslaved by Peacekeepers, later freed and bonded to the current Pilot.
2. **Rygel** — Carried the original Peacekeeper colony; now carries the exiled Rygel as its only passenger.
3. **Andala** — A peace-broker Leviathan.
4. **Talyn** — The first hybrid Leviathan/gunship, born from Moya.
5. **Rovhu** — A Leviathan whose Pilot had his arms eaten off multiple times (mentioned in the episode guide).[1]
