# Farscape Research Plan

## Goal

Build the most complete practical Farscape wiki possible, covering the television series and its production and fandom context without sacrificing source quality, provenance, or navigability.

## Research lanes

1. Episode-by-episode canon and production notes
2. Character biographies, arcs, relationships, and costume evolution
3. Species, worlds, factions, technology, ships, and terminology
4. Writers, directors, actors, puppeteers, designers, composers, and crew
5. Scripts, transcripts, deleted material, commentary, interviews, and conventions
6. Costume, makeup, creature design, miniatures, visual effects, and sound
7. Screenshots and promotional/behind-the-scenes media with provenance
8. Critical reception, preservation, fan communities, and the series legacy

## Source hierarchy

1. Episodes, official releases, credits, commentaries, and original interviews
2. Primary interviews and production archives
3. Reputable reference works and contemporary journalism
4. Established fan wikis and episode guides as leads, cross-checked before canon claims
5. Forums and social media only as leads or clearly labeled fandom testimony

## Vault workflow

- Work in bounded slices: one episode, one character, one production topic, or one source cluster per run.
- Before writing, search the existing vault and update the canonical note if it exists.
- Use descriptive stable filenames and YAML frontmatter.
- Add `[[wikilinks]]` while writing and update the relevant index.
- Keep raw source notes, claims, and synthesis distinguishable.
- Save images only when provenance and rights status are recorded; otherwise save a media record with a remote link and an acquisition note.
- Keep a research log with date, scope, sources consulted, notes created/updated, and unresolved questions.
- Never let an automated run rewrite a large set of existing notes without a clear bounded scope.

## Automation

- Research Scout's general morning routine runs at 08:00 and proposes/researches a topic across AJ's wider interests.
- The dedicated Farscape Wiki Curator runs at 09:00 daily and expands one bounded, source-grounded slice.
- Both routines deliver to the Research Scout Bot Chat and retain continuity with the previous run.
- The original Research Scout kickoff remains scheduled for 23:30 on 2026-09-15.

## Planned first pass

- Establish season and episode indexes.
- Build a canonical character index and start with John Crichton, Aeryn Sun, Ka D'Argo, and Chiana.
- Create production indexes for writers, directors, cast, creature design, costumes, and music.
- Create a media index and provenance register before collecting screenshots or promotional images.
- Use the freely available YouTube episodes as a viewing/transcript source where accessible, while recording the exact video URL and episode identification.

## Open questions

- Which YouTube channel/uploads are stable and authorized for each season?
- Which fan wikis provide the best episode-level leads, and where do they disagree?
- Which interviews and production archives are available in full text or video transcript form?
- What image sources can be safely linked, embedded, or locally archived under applicable rights?
