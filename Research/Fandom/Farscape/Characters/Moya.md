# Moya

## Overview

Moya is a Leviathan-class biomechanoid spaceship and the primary setting for the Farscape series. She is the third of five Leviathans produced by the ancient Builders, the others being the peace-broker Leviathans Rygel (carrying the Peacekeepers' original colony), Andala, and Talyn (a hybrid Peacekeeper/Leviathan warship).[1][2] Moya's character arc is one of the most emotionally significant in the series: from captured Peacekeeper transport to bonded companion of the crew, through pregnancy and birth of Talyn, and eventual liberation.[3]

## Canonical biography

### Pre-series and enslavement

Moya was originally captured by the Peacekeepers as a young Leviathan and fitted with a control collar. A Peacekeeper Pilot (later identified in flashbacks as the original Pilot) was bonded to her, and she served as a Peacekeeper transport ship. She was fitted with a control collar that the Peacekeepers used to suppress her natural Starburst capability, effectively silencing one of her most fundamental modes of movement and self-expression.[3]

During the pilot episode, Moya enters a Starburst to escape a Scarran attack. Crichton, a human astrophysicist, falls through the wormhole from Earth and lands in Moya's control deck. He is captured by Peacekeepers aboard a Prowler, and after escaping, he joins the crew already aboard Moya: John Crichton, Pilot (the current bonded Pilot), Chiana, Rygel, D'Argo, and Aeryn Sun.

### Season 1

Moya is the primary setting for the series. The control collar is removed early in Season 1 (episode 1.14 "Aprefect'surprise"), freeing Moya from Peacekeeper control.[3] The relationship between Moya, her Pilot, and the crew deepens throughout the season. In the Season 1 finale "I, et Cetera," Moya and Pilot's shared consciousness is revealed when a mind-transfer experiment connects all the crew members through the Pilot.[4]

### Season 2

In episode 2.10 "Look at the Princess – I Do, I Think," Moya is delivered to a Peacekeeper processing station. In 2.11 "Look at the Princess – The Maltese Crichton," the Builder Kahayn explains Moya's history and the Builders' creation of Leviathans as ambassadors of peace. In 2.12 "Look at the Princess – I Conclude, I Guess," Kahayn grants Moya verbal speech, which she uses to declare that she and Crichton are in love.[3]

The most pivotal event of Season 2 occurs in episode 2.18 "I,ETCetera" (S2.18), where Moya becomes pregnant. Peacekeeper Admiral Sullus Tal artificially impregnated Moya using Peacekeeper gunship (Dakkita-class) genetic material, creating the first-ever hybrid Leviathan/warship — Talyn. The pregnancy is depicted as a long and complicated process, with side effects including involuntary Starbursts, malfunctioning life support, and erratic DRD behavior.[5]

### Season 3

Moya gives birth to Talyn in episode 3.21 "I,ETCetera" (S3.21). Talyn is a unique hybrid — part Leviathan, part Peacekeeper gunship — and becomes a major recurring character in Seasons 3 and 4. Moya remains a companion vessel through the rest of Season 3, appearing in episodes including "Into the Lion's Den" (3.11–3.20) and "Dog with Two Bones" (3.22).

In the Season 3 finale "Dog with Two Bones," Moya is shown to have suffered significant damage. The peacekeeper forces pursue her, and she escapes with Crichton into the wormhole at the end of the episode.[3]

### The Peacekeeper Wars

Moya survives *The Peacekeeper Wars* and returns with the crew for Season 4. She continues to serve as the primary vessel throughout the final season.[3]

### Season 4

Moya is the primary setting for the final season. The crew returns to Moya after the events of the miniseries and the series continues with the crew navigating the universe from aboard the Leviathan.[3]

## Leviathan biology and capabilities

- **Starburst:** Moya's primary escape mechanism — a faster-than-travel method in which the Leviathan rides an energy stream.[2] Requires significant energy to replenish between uses.
- **Bioelectric field:** Keeps small hull openings from venting atmosphere.[6]
- **Life support:** Maintained by the bonded Pilot; major Malfunctions occur if the Pilot is incapacitated.
- **Self-repair:** Hodian trill bats (symbiotic organisms) excrete a sealant that repairs hull breaches.[6]
- **Pregnancy:** Moya can gestate embryos internally; the birth of Talyn demonstrated this capability.
- **No natural weaponry:** Leviathans were designed as transport ships and rely on Starburst for defense.
- **Command center:** Located at the core of the Leviathan with a viewing port. Pilot's Den is at the base of the command systems.
- **Internal structure:** Includes command decks, living quarters, workshops, hangar decks, life-support systems, and a Starburst chamber at the core.[2]
- **DRDs:** Moya's DRDs are yellow and largely benign, used for repairs, communication, and defensive combat.[7]

## Character traits and personality

Moya is described by the character Zhaan as "a bio-mechanoid; a living ship." Her relationship with the crew is maternal and protective. The crew often speaks of Moya in personal terms, and the series explores her emotions, desires, and pain.[3]

The Mind Cleansing episode (2.07 "Mind's Eye") is notable for Moya's ability to experience and process emotional trauma. In "I,ETCetera" (S2.18), the shared consciousness experience reveals Moya's deep feelings for Crichton and her desire for freedom from the Peacekeepers.
