# Production Credits: Chiana

## Scope

This is a partial, source-ranked credit ledger for the production personnel most directly connected with Chiana's makeup, hair, costume, creature design, and preserved costume records. It is not yet a definitive episode-by-episode end-credit transcription.

## Credit-handling rules

- Treat interview testimony as production evidence, but preserve who is speaking and where the testimony was published.
- Treat IMDb as a discovery and cross-check source, not as the final authority for episode-level credits.
- Treat Propstore costume descriptions as evidence about the preserved lot named on each catalogue page, not as a complete wardrobe ledger.
- Do not infer that a person credited on the series worked on every Chiana scene or costume.

## Related production evidence

The consulted interview/report connects the final makeup direction to Lesley Vanderwalt and discusses Dave Elsey's creature-design role, the rejected teeth, and the practical effects of contact lenses on Gigi Edgley's performance.[1] Propstore records independently document at least two Chiana costume groupings, including one explicitly labeled “Stunt,” which supports further screen-match work but does not establish episode assignment.[3][7]
