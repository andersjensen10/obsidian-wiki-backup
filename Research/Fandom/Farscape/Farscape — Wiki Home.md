## Coverage map

- **Episodes:** episode guides, synopses, credits, continuity, themes, notable dialogue, production notes, and stills.
- **Characters:** biographies, arcs, relationships, appearances, quotations, performers, and costume evolution.
- **Worldbuilding:** species, planets, factions, technology, spacecraft, languages, religion, politics, and terminology.
- **Production:** writers, directors, actors, designers, puppetry, costumes, visual effects, music, interviews, and production history.
- **Media:** screenshots, promotional images, behind-the-scenes material, interview media, and provenance/licensing notes.
- **Fandom:** fan wikis, conventions, fan scholarship, preservation, critical reception, and community history.

## Editorial standards

- Prefer primary sources and cite every externally sourced factual claim.
- Treat fan wikis as discovery tools and cross-check important claims against episode credits, transcripts, interviews, official releases, or multiple independent sources.
- Keep canon, production fact, interpretation, and fandom report visibly separate.
- Use one note per durable subject; update existing notes instead of creating duplicates.
- Add frontmatter, aliases, backlinks, and a Sources section to substantive notes.
- Preserve uncertainty rather than silently resolving contradictions.
- Media entries must record the source URL, creator/rightsholder when known, date accessed, and license or fair-use rationale. Prefer linking or embedding remote sources over duplicating copyrighted media locally.
