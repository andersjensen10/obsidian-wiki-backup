# Guided Narrator Agency & Persistent NPC Development

## Vision
Narrator intelligence must move beyond pacing into guided storycraft: it should create events, opportunities, consequences, and supporting characters that feel situated in the World while preserving the player's agency. NPCs should develop relationships, emotions, flaws, motivations, and memories across scenes rather than acting as disposable dialogue sources.

## Goal
The narrator advances configured World Sessions through grounded scene interventions and can introduce/manage persistent NPCs whose state and relationships evolve across scenes according to World rules, player actions, established memory, and narrative controls.

## Success metric
In a multi-scene scenario, the narrator produces contextually grounded events and NPC choices; NPC relationships and consequences remain recognizable in the next scene, while user evaluation can distinguish guided agency from arbitrary interruption.
