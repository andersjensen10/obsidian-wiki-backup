# Farscape World Flagship Pilot

## Vision
The Farscape World is the bounded flagship pilot that proves the World Session, narrator, character continuity, media, and presentation direction against a real narrative setting rather than abstract demos. It should use researched, locally documented canon and prepared assets, while not blocking on fully automated voice extraction.

## Goal
Create a validated Farscape-themed World Session pilot with curated local reference material, foundational characters, scene-ready narrative configuration, and the appropriate media/voice assets needed to exercise the integrated product direction.

## Success metric
An internal user can configure and play multiple coherent Farscape scenes with grounded characters and references, retain resulting World/Scene media, and complete the cross-scene validation scenario; manual prepared voice assets are acceptable where automation is not ready.
