# Configurable Narrative Control Surface

## Vision
World settings are creative instructions, not an engineering panel. Users should be able to understand and adjust how the narrator intervenes and how a World behaves without losing immersion, while the underlying interaction model remains consistent across genres.

## Goal
Each World exposes a coherent, editable control surface for narrator style/intervention, pacing, character autonomy, off-screen world change, story dimensions, consequences, improvisation range, and immutable "never do this" rules; changes take effect without breaking continuity.

## Success metric
A user can inspect and adjust every stated narrative dimension in an active World, understands the effect through in-world-oriented language and presentation, and sees subsequent narration honor the changed controls across scene transitions.
