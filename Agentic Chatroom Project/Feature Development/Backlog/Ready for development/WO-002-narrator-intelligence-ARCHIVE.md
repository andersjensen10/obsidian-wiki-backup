## Vision
Today's Narrator tooling is thin and lacks real judgment about pacing —
when to push a scene forward versus let it breathe. The Board calls this
out as coupled work to Worlds & Scenes, not a trailing follow-up: without
it, Worlds & Scenes will feel mechanical rather than alive.

## Goal
Narrator pacing behavior that's configurable per World or per Narrator
persona, replacing today's fixed/implicit pacing logic.

## Success metric
A pacing setting is user-configurable in a Narrator/World profile and
observably changes when the narrator advances a scene in a live test; ships
in step with initial Worlds & Scenes support, not after it.
