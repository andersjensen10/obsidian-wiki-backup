# Cross-Scene Experience Validation & Public-Readiness Evidence

## Vision
The existing scenario harness is the beginning of product evidence, not merely another functional check. The next evaluation must test whether a configured World Session remains coherent as narrative, memory, relationships, state, boundaries, and user controls accumulate across scenes.

## Goal
Extend experience validation with structured, repeatable multi-scene World Session scenarios and decision-ready metrics/evidence for whether the integrated experience is ready for non-AJ user testing.

## Success metric
A repeatable scenario covers configuration through multiple scenes and measures continuity, boundary adherence, narrator/NPC agency, control-change behavior, and usability observations; its output clearly identifies the public-testing recommendation and remaining risks without representing infrastructure failures as product passes.
