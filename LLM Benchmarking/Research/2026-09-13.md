## Selection rationale

Qwen3-Coder leads on the weighted score because it directly targets the program's stated gap—local programming and agentic coding—while staying close to the Qwen baseline's compactness and general instruct use. Its recommended Q4_K_M is smaller than Qwen3-32B's comparable quant, and the card provides native context, Apache licensing, GGUF metadata, an imatrix quant ladder, and an explicit llama.cpp launch path.[1][2] It is the only candidate this cycle with both strong coding-specific evidence and a low enough storage/fit risk to justify the next nightly preflight.

## Blockers and required preflight

- GPU memory is currently reported as `N/A`; do not download or launch until the runner confirms available unified/GPU memory and a safe context/parallelism profile.

- The selection is not a benchmark result and does not authorize promotion or production replacement.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Qwen3-32B:** credible and eligible, but lower coding/concurrency/feasibility score than Qwen3-Coder; retain as fallback if the Coder chat template or runner preflight fails.[3]

- **gpt-oss-120b:** unusually strong momentum and agentic feature claims, but the 62.8 GB GGUF, Harmony-format dependency, unknown context in the retrieved card, and unverified Spark fit make it unsuitable for this night's selection.[6][7]
