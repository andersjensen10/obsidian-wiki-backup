# Candidate research snapshot — 2026-09-14

## Decision

**Selected exactly one candidate for the next available nightly:** `Qwen/Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B-Instruct`, using the stable `AtomicChat/Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B-GGUF` release and `qwen3-coder-30b-a3b-Q4_K_M.gguf` (~18.56 GB).[1][2][8] This is a research selection only: no model was downloaded and no benchmark was run.[unverified]

The candidate meets policy: 30.5B total / 3.3B active MoE parameters, 262,144-token context, Apache-2.0 licensing, an imatrix GGUF ladder, and explicit llama.cpp support.[1] The base card reports agentic coding, browser-use, tool-calling, 623,620 downloads in the last month, and 1.24k likes; the GGUF release was updated 2026-07-22.[1]

## Baseline and readiness

The documented comparison baseline is `qwen3.8-27b-aggressive-q5` on Spark llama.cpp at `192.168.0.139:8014`, with two parallel slots.[unverified] This is internal operational context, not an external quality claim.[unverified]

Read-only preflight found Toshiba mounted read-write at `/media/aj/TOSHIBA EXT1`, 1.9 TB total, 911 GB free (48% free), so the policy's 20%-free storage floor is met.[unverified] The model library's `models/` directory is currently empty; the selected model is therefore **not acquired or runner-ready**.[unverified] The isolated E2E profile exists, the runner's `verify_run.py` exists, and `nvidia-smi` reports GPU memory as `N/A`.[unverified] Acquisition and launch must remain guarded until the runner confirms fit, checksum, profile, and headroom.[unverified] No production service was changed.[unverified]

## Selection rationale

Qwen3-Coder remains the highest-scoring policy-compliant option because it directly targets the benchmark program's coding and agentic gap while keeping a Qwen-like ~19 GB quantization footprint.[unverified] Its model and GGUF cards provide the required context, license, format, quantization metadata, and llama.cpp path.[1][8] Qwen3-32B is the credible fallback if the Coder template or runner preflight fails; the larger candidates are not eligible until their context and Spark fit are established.[unverified]

## Blockers and next preflight

- `models/` is empty: the selected candidate is not locally ready; this report authorizes no download by itself.[unverified]
- GPU memory is reported as `N/A`; confirm actual unified/GPU memory and safe context/parallelism before acquisition or launch.[unverified]

- This snapshot is not a benchmark result and does not authorize promotion.[unverified]

## Rejected alternatives

- **Qwen3-32B:** eligible, but lower coding/agentic and concurrency/feasibility score than Qwen3-Coder; retain as fallback.[3][9]
- **GLM-4.5-Air:** credible agent/coding candidate with available GGUFs, but policy eligibility fails because the retrieved card does not establish context length; it is also materially heavier.[4][10]
- **gpt-oss-120b:** compelling general/agentic capability and permissive license, but the retrieved evidence does not establish context or Spark fit, and the multi-file 120B deployment is operationally riskier.[6][11]
