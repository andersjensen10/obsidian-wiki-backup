## Selection rationale

Qwen3.6 remains the single recommended next benchmark candidate, not a completed selection. It combines the strongest current coding/agent evidence among the operationally documented candidates, 3B-active MoE efficiency, Apache-2.0 licensing, current imatrix GGUF availability, MTP support, and a verified prior SSD artifact.[1] Ornith has the strongest reported coding scores, but its captured card requires a heavier 2×80 GB deployment for the source model and does not provide sufficient license/runtime evidence for this Spark cycle.[5] GLM-4.7-Flash is a credible smaller fallback, but its GGUF card documents a fixed-gating correction and updated-runtime requirement.[4]

## Blockers and rejected alternatives

- **Current selection blocked:** the same-day readiness record reports a partial acquisition (`4,464,275,456` of `22,663,387,424` expected bytes), so no completion or checksum claim is made.[unverified]
- **Runner capacity blocked:** fresh isolated CUDA/MPS fit could not be verified because MPS was not ready; `/health` alone is insufficient proof of GPU backing.[unverified]
