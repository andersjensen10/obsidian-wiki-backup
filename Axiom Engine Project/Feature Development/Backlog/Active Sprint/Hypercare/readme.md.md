Post-ship watch lane, owned by the Scrummaster. A story lands here the moment its `status` flips to `shipped`, and stays through the entirety of the following sprint while the Senior QA Manager watches it for regressions or fresh reports. When the window closes, the Scrummaster sanitizes and archives it into `Published features/Sprint N - Week WW`.

Starts empty.
